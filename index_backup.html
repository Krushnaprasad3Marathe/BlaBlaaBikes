Help centre
How do I book a ride?

Open Find a ride, search by area, and open a ride to see its pickup point, notes and rider details. Then choose Request to Join and agree the exact meeting spot in the chat.

How do I offer a ride?

Go to Offer a ride, enter your route, landmarks, time, contribution and bike details, drop the pin on the map where you will meet, and publish.

How is the price decided?

The rider sets a contribution per passenger. It is meant to share fuel costs, not to make a profit.

What about helmets and safety?

Each ride shows whether a spare helmet is provided. If it is not, bring your own. Confirm the pickup point in chat and ask the rider to keep to a comfortable speed.

How do I get started?

Sign up with your name, email and local area, then add your motorcycle in Profile if you plan to offer rides.
